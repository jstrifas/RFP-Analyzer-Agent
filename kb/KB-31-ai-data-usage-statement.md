# AI & Data Usage Statement

**Last updated:** June 1, 2026
**Classification:** Public

---

Customers regularly ask how Tidewell uses artificial intelligence and whether their data is used to train AI models. This statement answers those questions.

**Does Tidewell use customer data to train AI or machine learning models?**
**No.** Tidewell does not use customer data, including records synced through Tidewell Sync or data explored in Tidewell Insights, to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI or machine learning model, whether Tidewell's own or a third party's. This commitment is also written into our Data Processing Addendum.

**Do Tidewell products include generative AI features?**
Tidewell Sync and Tidewell Insights do not currently include generative AI features that process customer data. If we add such features in the future, we will:
- Update this statement and our Subprocessor List before launch
- Give customers at least 30 days' notice of any new subprocessor involved
- Make the features optional, so customers can choose whether to use them

**Do Tidewell employees use AI tools?**
Yes, for internal work such as writing code and documents. Employees may only use AI tools approved by the Security team, and our Acceptable Use Policy prohibits entering customer data into unapproved AI tools. Approved tools are reviewed through our vendor review process before use.

**Do you use aggregated product usage data?**
We use aggregated and de-identified information about how the Tidewell application is used, such as which features are used most, to improve our products. This does not include the contents of customer data.
