# Physical Security Statement

**Last updated:** March 1, 2026
**Owner:** Security & Compliance
**Classification:** Public

---

**Data centers.** Tidewell does not own or operate any data centers. All Tidewell production systems and customer data are hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). Physical security of the data centers, including facility access controls, surveillance, environmental protections, and secure disposal of storage media, is the responsibility of AWS under the AWS shared responsibility model. AWS's physical security controls are independently audited, and customers can review AWS's audit reports through AWS Artifact.

**Offices.** Tidewell is a remote-first company. Tidewell does not store customer data in any office, and no Tidewell office hosts servers or network equipment used for production services. Where Tidewell uses shared office space for meetings or team events, access to Tidewell systems still requires the same authentication and device controls as remote work.

**Devices.** Because personnel work remotely, device security takes the place of traditional office controls. All Tidewell-issued laptops are enrolled in device management, use full-disk encryption, lock automatically when idle, and can be locked and wiped remotely. See the Acceptable Use Policy and the Endpoint & Asset Management Standard.

**Paper records.** Tidewell does not routinely keep customer data on paper.
