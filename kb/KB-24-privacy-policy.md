# Tidewell Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 1, 2026
**Classification:** Public

---

This Privacy Policy explains how Tidewell Data, Inc. ("Tidewell," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information, and the choices and rights available to you.

## 1. Two Kinds of Data

**Customer data.** When a business uses Tidewell Sync or Tidewell Insights, it connects its own systems to Tidewell, and data flows through our service on its behalf. We call this **customer data**. For customer data, the business customer is in control: it decides what data is synced and why. Tidewell acts as a **service provider** (a "processor" under GDPR) and handles customer data only to provide the service, under our agreement and Data Processing Addendum with that customer. If your personal information is part of a Tidewell customer's data and you want to exercise your privacy rights, please contact that customer directly. We will help them respond.

**Information we collect for our own purposes.** We also collect personal information to run our business, such as when you visit our website, sign up for an account, contact sales or support, or attend an event. For this information, Tidewell is the **business** (a "controller" under GDPR). The rest of this policy mainly covers this second category.

## 2. Information We Collect

- **Account information:** name, work email, company, job title, and password or SSO identity.
- **Billing information:** billing contact details and invoices.
- **Usage information:** how you use the Tidewell application, such as features used, sign-in times, and IP address.
- **Communications:** messages you send to sales or support.
- **Website information:** pages visited, referring site, and device and browser type, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:
- Provide, maintain, and secure the Tidewell service
- Create and manage accounts and process billing
- Respond to questions and provide support
- Send service announcements and, where permitted, marketing emails (you can unsubscribe at any time)
- Improve our products using aggregated and de-identified usage information
- Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- Comply with legal obligations

**We do not use customer data to train AI or machine learning models, and we do not sell personal information.**

## 4. How We Share Information

We share personal information only:
- With **subprocessors and service providers** that help us run Tidewell, under contracts that require them to protect it. Our current subprocessors are listed on our Subprocessor List.
- When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Tidewell, our customers, or others
- As part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to affected users

## 5. Where Information Is Stored

Customer data is stored in the hosting region the customer selects: the United States (AWS us-east-1) or the European Union (AWS eu-west-1). Information we collect for our own purposes is primarily stored in the United States. Where personal information is transferred out of the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid transfer mechanism.

## 6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, then delete it. Customer data is deleted within 30 days after a customer's subscription ends. Billing records are kept for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes.

## 7. Security

We protect personal information and customer data with encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, logging and monitoring, and regular security testing. We maintain a SOC 2 Type II report. More information is available on our Trust Center.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; Tidewell does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To make a request, email **privacy@tidewelldata.example**. We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by law.

## 9. Children

Tidewell is a business service and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will notify account holders by email or through the service before the changes take effect.
