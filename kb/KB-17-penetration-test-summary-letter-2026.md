# Penetration Test Summary Letter

**Brightline Security, LLC**
**Date:** May 29, 2026
**Classification:** Confidential — shared on request

---

**To:** Tidewell Data, Inc.
**Re:** 2026 Annual Penetration Test — Summary of Results

Brightline Security, LLC ("Brightline") was engaged by Tidewell Data, Inc. ("Tidewell") to perform an independent penetration test of the Tidewell platform.

**Testing window:** April 20, 2026 – May 8, 2026

**In-scope targets:**
- The Tidewell web application (Tidewell Sync and Tidewell Insights)
- The Tidewell public API
- Internet-facing infrastructure supporting the production environments in AWS us-east-1 and eu-west-1

**Summary of results**

Brightline identified a total of 9 findings during the engagement:

| Severity | Findings identified | Open as of this letter |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | 0 | 0 |
| High | 1 | 0 |
| Medium | 3 | 1 |
| Low | 3 | 2 |
| Informational | 2 | 2 |

The single High-severity finding was remediated by Tidewell and verified as fixed by Brightline during retesting on May 26, 2026.

As of the date of this letter, **there are no open Critical or High findings.** The remaining open Medium and Low findings are being tracked by Tidewell for remediation according to its vulnerability management timeframes.

This letter summarizes the results of the engagement as of the date above. It does not provide assurance about the security of the Tidewell platform at any other time.

Sincerely,

Engagement Lead
Brightline Security, LLC
