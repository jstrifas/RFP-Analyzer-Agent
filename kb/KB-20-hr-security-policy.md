# HR Security Policy

**Document version:** 1.3
**Effective date:** February 1, 2026
**Policy owner:** VP of People, with the Head of Security
**Parent policy:** Information Security Policy
**Review cycle:** Annual
**Classification:** Internal — may be shared with customers on request

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the security requirements that apply to Tidewell personnel before, during, and after their time at Tidewell.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Tidewell employees, contractors, and interns.

## 3. Before Joining

3.1 **Background checks** are completed for all new hires before their start date, where permitted by local law. Checks include identity verification and employment history, and may include criminal record checks depending on the role and location.

3.2 Contractors with access to Restricted data are subject to the same background check requirements as employees, either directly or through their employer.

3.3 Offers are made conditional on satisfactory background check results.

## 4. On Joining

4.1 Before receiving access to any Tidewell system, new personnel must:
- Sign a confidentiality agreement
- Acknowledge the Information Security Policy and the Acceptable Use Policy

4.2 New personnel complete **security awareness training** within their first two weeks.

## 5. During Employment

5.1 All personnel complete **security awareness training annually**. Training covers data handling, password and MFA practices, recognizing social engineering, and how to report a security concern.

5.2 Engineers also complete secure coding training, as described in the Secure SDLC Policy.

5.3 Training completion is tracked. Personnel who have not completed annual training within 30 days of the due date have their system access reviewed by their manager.

5.4 Personnel must report suspected security incidents immediately, as described in the Incident Response Plan.

## 6. Role Changes

When personnel change roles, their manager requests removal of access no longer needed, as described in the Access Control Policy.

## 7. Leaving Tidewell

7.1 All system access is removed **within 24 hours** of the person's last working day. For involuntary departures, access is removed at or before the time the person is notified.

7.2 Company devices must be returned. Devices that are not returned within 14 days are remotely locked and wiped.

7.3 Departing personnel are reminded of their continuing confidentiality obligations.

## 8. Disciplinary Process

Violations of Tidewell security policies are handled through the standard disciplinary process and may result in termination of employment or contract.
