# Data Processing Addendum (Excerpt)

**Tidewell Data, Inc. — Standard Data Processing Addendum**
**Version:** May 2026
**Classification:** Public

*This excerpt reproduces the operative sections of Tidewell's standard Data Processing Addendum ("DPA"). The full DPA, including the Standard Contractual Clauses and all annexes, is incorporated into the Tidewell Subscription Agreement.*

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Agreement"** means the Tidewell Subscription Agreement between Customer and Tidewell.

1.2 **"Customer Personal Data"** means any Personal Data contained in customer data that Tidewell processes on behalf of Customer under the Agreement.

1.3 **"Data Protection Laws"** means all laws that apply to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), each as amended.

1.4 **"Personal Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data processed by Tidewell.

1.5 **"Subprocessor"** means any third party engaged by Tidewell to process Customer Personal Data.

Terms such as "Controller," "Processor," "Data Subject," "Personal Data," and "Processing" have the meanings given in the GDPR.

## 2. Roles and Scope

2.1 Customer is the Controller, and Tidewell is the Processor, of Customer Personal Data.

2.2 Tidewell will process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions, which include the Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's configuration of the Tidewell services. Tidewell will inform Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction violates Data Protection Laws.

2.3 Tidewell will not sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined under the CCPA, and will not use Customer Personal Data to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

## 3. Confidentiality

Tidewell will ensure that all personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

## 4. Security

4.1 Tidewell will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Personal Data, as described in Annex II (Security Measures). These include encryption of Customer Personal Data at rest and in transit, access controls based on least privilege, logging and monitoring, and regular security testing.

4.2 Tidewell may update its security measures from time to time, provided that updates do not materially reduce the overall level of protection.

## 5. Subprocessors

5.1 Customer authorizes Tidewell to engage the Subprocessors listed on Tidewell's Subprocessor List, available on the Tidewell Trust Center.

5.2 Tidewell will give Customer at least **30 days' notice** before adding or replacing a Subprocessor, by updating the Subprocessor List and notifying customers who have subscribed to updates. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that notice period.

5.3 Tidewell will impose data protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains responsible for its Subprocessors' performance.

## 6. Personal Data Breach

6.1 Tidewell will notify Customer **without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours**, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach.

6.2 The notification will describe, to the extent known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

6.3 Tidewell will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate, and mitigate the effects of the Personal Data Breach and will provide Customer with reasonable assistance in meeting Customer's own notification obligations under Data Protection Laws.

6.4 Tidewell's notification of a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

Tidewell will promptly forward to Customer any request it receives from a Data Subject relating to Customer Personal Data and will not respond directly except on Customer's instructions. Tidewell will provide reasonable assistance to help Customer respond to such requests.

## 8. International Transfers

8.1 Customer Personal Data of customers hosted in the EU region is stored in AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland).

8.2 Where Customer Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module Two: Controller to Processor) incorporated into this DPA apply.

## 9. Audits

9.1 Tidewell will make available to Customer, on request and subject to confidentiality obligations, its most recent SOC 2 Type II report and bridge letter.

9.2 If the SOC 2 report does not reasonably address Customer's audit needs, or where required by Data Protection Laws or a regulator, Customer may conduct an audit at Customer's expense, no more than once per 12-month period, on at least 30 days' written notice, during normal business hours, and in a way that does not disrupt Tidewell's operations.

## 10. Return and Deletion

10.1 On termination of the Agreement, Tidewell will delete Customer Personal Data **within 30 days**, unless Data Protection Laws require Tidewell to keep it.

10.2 Customer may export its data during that 30-day period by contacting Tidewell support.

10.3 Copies in backups are deleted as backups expire, in line with Tidewell's backup retention schedule.

10.4 Tidewell will certify deletion in writing on request.

## 11. Order of Precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the Agreement, this DPA governs with respect to the processing of Customer Personal Data. If the Standard Contractual Clauses conflict with this DPA, the Standard Contractual Clauses govern.
