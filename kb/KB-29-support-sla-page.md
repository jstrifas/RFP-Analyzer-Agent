# Support & Service Level Agreement

**Last updated:** April 1, 2026
**Audience:** Customers and prospects
**Classification:** Public

---

## Uptime Commitment

Tidewell commits to **99.9% monthly uptime** for the Tidewell Sync and Tidewell Insights applications and API for customers on the **Business** and **Enterprise** plans.

**The Team plan does not include an uptime commitment or service credits.**

### How uptime is measured

Uptime is the percentage of minutes in a calendar month when the Tidewell application and API are available. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 business days in advance is not counted as downtime. Delays in syncs caused by a customer's source system or warehouse are also not counted.

### Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, eligible customers can request a service credit:

| Monthly uptime | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% | 10% of that month's subscription fee |
| Below 99.5% | 25% of that month's subscription fee |
| Below 99.0% | 50% of that month's subscription fee |

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Credits are the sole remedy for missing the uptime commitment, unless an Enterprise agreement says otherwise.

## Support by Plan

| | Team | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support channels | Email | Email, in-app chat | Email, in-app chat, phone |
| Support hours | Business hours (US Eastern) | Business hours in your hosting region | Business hours in your hosting region, plus urgent-issue escalation outside business hours |
| First response — Urgent | 1 business day | 4 business hours | 1 hour |
| First response — Normal | 2 business days | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| Named customer success manager | — | — | ✓ |

**Urgent** means Tidewell is unavailable, or all syncs for a workspace are failing, with no workaround.

## System Status

Real-time and historical status is published on the Tidewell status page. Customers can subscribe to status updates by email.
