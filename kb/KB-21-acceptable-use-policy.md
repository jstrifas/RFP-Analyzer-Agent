# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document version:** 1.2
**Effective date:** February 1, 2026
**Policy owner:** Head of Security
**Parent policy:** Information Security Policy
**Review cycle:** Annual
**Classification:** Internal

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Tidewell personnel may and may not use Tidewell devices, systems, and data.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all Tidewell employees, contractors, and interns, and to all Tidewell-issued devices, accounts, and systems.

## 3. General Use

3.1 Tidewell systems are provided for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with work, break the law, or put Tidewell data at risk.

3.2 Personnel must protect their credentials. Passwords, MFA devices, and API keys must never be shared.

3.3 Personnel must lock their screens when leaving their devices unattended.

## 4. Devices

4.1 Tidewell work must be done on **Tidewell-issued laptops** enrolled in device management.

4.2 Personnel must not disable or bypass security software, full-disk encryption, or automatic updates.

4.3 Personal phones may be used for company email and chat only through Tidewell's approved mobile apps with a device passcode enabled. Customer data must never be stored on a personal device.

4.4 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the Security team immediately.

## 5. Handling Customer Data

5.1 Personnel may access customer data only when needed for their role and only through approved tools, as described in the Access Control Policy.

5.2 Customer data must never be:
- Downloaded to a laptop unless required for an approved support or incident task, and deleted once the task is complete
- Sent through personal email, personal cloud storage, or personal messaging apps
- Pasted into chat channels, tickets, or documents beyond what the task requires
- Entered into AI tools that are not approved by the Security team

## 6. Approved Software and AI Tools

6.1 Personnel may install software only from Tidewell's approved catalog. Requests for new software go through the vendor review process.

6.2 Only AI tools approved by the Security team may be used with Internal, Confidential, or Restricted information. The approved list is published on the internal wiki.

## 7. Prohibited Activities

Personnel must not:
- Access systems or data they are not authorized to use
- Attempt to test or probe Tidewell systems for vulnerabilities without Security team approval
- Use Tidewell systems for illegal activity, harassment, or to send spam
- Install unapproved remote-access tools

## 8. Monitoring

Tidewell monitors its systems and devices for security purposes, in line with applicable law. Personnel should not expect privacy when using Tidewell systems.

## 9. Reporting

Suspected violations of this policy or security concerns must be reported to the Security team.
